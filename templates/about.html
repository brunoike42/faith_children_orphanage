Foundation of faith children's Orphanage Uganda

Faith Children’s Orphanage Uganda is a community-driven organization dedicated to caring for vulnerable and orphaned children. We provide shelter, education, healthcare, and emotional support to help every child grow into a confident and independent individual.

Our mission is to provide a safe, loving, and supportive environment for orphaned and vulnerable children by meeting their basic needs, promoting education, and nurturing their physical, emotional, and spiritual growth.

To create a future where every child, regardless of their background, has the opportunity to live with dignity, access quality education, and reach their full potential.
