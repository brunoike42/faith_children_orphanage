About Us

Faith Children's Orphanage

Faith Children’s Orphanage Uganda was founded with a simple but heartfelt purpose: to respond to the growing need for care and protection of vulnerable children in our community. What started as a small effort to help a few children has grown into a mission that continues to touch many lives. Every child who walks through our doors brings a unique story—but they all share one thing in common: the need for love, care, and a chance to begin again. And that is exactly what we strive to give.

Our Mission is to provide a safe, nurturing, and loving home for orphaned and vulnerable children by meeting their physical, emotional, and educational needs, while empowering them to grow into confident, independent, and hopeful individuals.
